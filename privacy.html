1. Introduction

Welcome to VocabFix. VocabFix is an English vocabulary learning application designed to help users improve their English vocabulary through organized learning categories, quizzes and progress tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use VocabFix.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, VocabFix may process:

  • Google Account information required for authentication.
  • Learning data, including Learned Words, Bookmarks and Quiz History.
  • Technical information required to operate application services.

3. Google Sign-In

VocabFix uses Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication. Authentication is handled through Google's and Firebase's services. VocabFix does not ask users to provide their Google account passwords directly to the application.

4. Firebase and Cloud Backup

VocabFix uses Google Firebase services to support authentication and cloud data storage.

Cloud Backup may store the following learning information:

  • Learned Words
  • Bookmarks
  • Quiz History

Active Quiz State is not included in Cloud Backup.

5. Local Data Storage

Certain learning information may be stored locally on your device to support application functionality. Local data may remain until you clear application data, uninstall the application or delete the relevant information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through VocabFix, including the use of Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules. However, no electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

VocabFix uses Google services, including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Hosting. Other integrated third-party components, if enabled, may process technical information according to their respective privacy policies.

Private learning records are not intentionally made publicly available. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate application features.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may manage locally stored learning information where supported. For assistance with accessing or requesting deletion of cloud-stored information, please contact us.

10. Children's Privacy

VocabFix is an educational application. Parents or guardians may contact us if they have concerns about a child's personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, technology or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published on our official website.
